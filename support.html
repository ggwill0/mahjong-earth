Mahjong Earth — Support

Need help? Email Ggwill0@me.com and include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap two identical tiles to remove them. A tile can be picked only when nothing is on top of it and its left or right side is open. Brighter tiles are free; dimmed tiles are blocked. Clear every tile to win.

Can every game be won?

Yes. Every deal is built backwards from an empty board, so it always has a solution. If you get stuck, Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles into a layout that can still be cleared, and Undo steps back as far as you like. Both are free and unlimited, and so is Hint.

Is there a timer?

The clock only records your time for your stats. It never ends a game or forces you to hurry, and you can hide it in Settings.

Which tile packs are free?

When you first open the app you choose one pack free: Land Mammals, Sea Creatures, Birds, Insects, Humans or Aliens. You pick before your first game (there is no separate starter set). Each pack has 36 different hand-drawn tiles and its own animated background; tap See all 36 tiles to preview a pack first. The choice is permanent, so take your time. Other packs are US$0.99 each (prices vary by country). Bought packs before? Tap Restore Purchases on the same screen.

Are there ads?

No. Mahjong Earth has no ads at all, on any device. You never have to watch anything to undo, shuffle or get a hint.

What are the Layout Packs?

Two one-time US$0.99 purchases, each adding 5 more layouts. Layout Pack 1: Pyramid, Rainbow, Lotus, Pagoda and Fortress. Layout Pack 2: Archipelago, Snowflake, Hourglass, Volcano and Waterfall. Find them below the free boards on the Layouts screen or in the Tile Sets store. The 10 original layouts are always free.

Can I skip the opening animation?

Yes, tap anywhere to skip it. With Reduce Motion turned on (Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Motion) it's replaced by a simple fade.

Can I change the music or sound volume?

Yes: Settings ▸ Sound & Music has separate volume sliders for music and sound effects (effects start at 50 %), plus on/off switches. The music button on the menu and in a game turns music off and on.

I bought a pack (or a Layout Pack) on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases (or the Restore button on the Tile Packs screen). Your free pick is stored on each device and is not restored through the App Store.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

I lost my stats.

Stats and saved games are stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them, and we can't recover them because we never receive them.

The effects are too much.

Turn on Settings ▸ Reduce motion. The game also follows the system Reduce Motion setting.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the music button (♪) on the main menu or at the top of the game screen, or switch off Settings ▸ Music. The music is generated on your device, mixes with your own music or podcasts, and follows the silent switch. If something else is already playing when you open the app, the music starts muted.

How do I stop the notifications?

There are two, both created on your device (no push service, nothing collected): a daily reminder (7 PM by default; you can change the time) and a one-time new pack alert when an update adds a tile pack. Switch either off in Settings ▸ Notifications, or turn off all notifications for Mahjong Earth in the system Settings.
