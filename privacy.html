Privacy Policy — Mahjong Earth

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Not legal advice. This policy was written to describe how the app actually works. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional if you have any doubt about your legal obligations.

Mahjong Earth ("the app") is a mahjong solitaire game for iPhone, iPad and Mac made by William Lankenau ("we", "us"). It is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not listed in the App Store Kids Category.

The short version

  • We collect no personal information, and nothing is tracked. No accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no analytics.
  • The app has no ads and contains no advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • Your games, stats, settings and your free tile-pack choice are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, statistics (games played and won, best times, streaks), settings (including sound and music volume), purchases cache and the free pack you chose are saved on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. We cannot see this data and it is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Tile packs and Layout Packs are one-time In-App Purchases sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. Apple only tells the app which items you own. Apple's privacy policy covers purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

No advertising, no tracking

The app shows no ads of any kind and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), App Tracking Transparency, analytics services or any third-party SDK. It does not share data with anyone, because it doesn't collect any.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder and the one-time "new tile pack" alert are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push service, no device token and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app reports your wins, best time and achievements to Game Center. That service is provided by Apple under your Game Center account and Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive or store any Game Center data ourselves.

Your California privacy rights

We are based in California. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not collect, sell or share personal information and we do not hold personal information about you. Questions can be sent to the email below, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
